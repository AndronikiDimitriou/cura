💊 Cura

Cura is a free personal app for medicine reminders and your health record: medicines and doses, refill and expiry warnings, lab results with charts, hearing tests, measurements, vaccines, appointments and health history. It is made by Androniki Dimitriou for family and friends.

Everything you enter stays on your own phone. If you turn on the optional automatic backup, Cura saves a copy to your own Google Drive.
