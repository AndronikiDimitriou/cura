Cura · Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

What Cura stores

Everything you enter in Cura (medicines, doses, health history, documents, measurements, vaccines, appointments) is stored only on your own phone. There is no Cura server and no account. The maker of Cura cannot see your data.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose “Connect Google Drive”, you sign in with Google and Cura asks only for the permission drive.file: it can create and read only the files Cura itself made — a backup file of your Cura data in a folder called “Cura backups” in your own Google Drive. Cura cannot see, change or delete any other file in your Drive. The backup goes directly from your phone to your Google Drive; it is not sent anywhere else. You can disconnect at any time in Cura (Profile ▸ Automatic backup) and delete the backups from your Drive yourself.

Cura's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Reading documents with Claude (optional)

If you add your own Anthropic API key and scan a document or photo, that document is sent to Anthropic's Claude to be read, using your key. Nothing is sent unless you scan something.

No ads, no tracking

Cura has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and does not sell or share data.
