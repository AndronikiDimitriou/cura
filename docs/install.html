Android

No computer needed. No 7-day expiry.

Download Cura.apk
  1. Open the download link on your Android phone.
  2. Download the APK file.
  3. Tap the downloaded Cura.apk file.
  4. If Android asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for the app you opened it with (Chrome, Files, Gmail, etc.).
  5. Go back and tap Install.
  6. Open Cura. Done!

Android may display a security warning because Cura is installed outside Google Play.

iPhone

Free installation using AltStore. You need a Mac or Windows computer.

Download Cura.ipa
Important: The first installation needs a USB cable connecting your iPhone to your computer.

What you need

  • An iPhone with a passcode enabled.
  • A Mac or Windows PC.
  • A USB cable that supports data transfer.
  • A free Apple Account.
  • Wi-Fi, with the iPhone and computer on the same network.

Part 1 — Install AltServer on your computer

macOS

  1. Download AltServer for macOS from the official AltStore website.
  2. Move AltServer to Applications and open it.
  3. AltServer has no window. It appears as a small diamond icon in the menu bar (top right). If you can't see it, the menu bar may be full: quit a few menu bar apps.

Windows

  1. Install iTunes and iCloud from Apple's website. Do not use the Microsoft Store versions.
  2. Download and install AltServer for Windows from the official AltStore website.
  3. Open AltServer. Its icon appears in the system tray (bottom right).

Part 2 — Install AltStore on your iPhone

  1. Connect your iPhone to the computer using USB.
  2. Unlock your iPhone and tap Trust if requested.
  3. Click the AltServer icon → Install AltStore → choose your iPhone.
  4. Sign in with your Apple Account and wait until it finishes.

Part 3 — Trust AltStore

  1. On your iPhone, open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.
  2. Tap your Apple Account and tap Trust.
  3. If requested, enable Developer Mode under Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode. Your iPhone will restart.
  4. Open AltStore and sign in with the same Apple Account.

Part 4 — Install Cura

  1. On your iPhone, open this page in Safari and tap Download Cura.ipa above.
  2. Open AltStore → My Apps → tap +.
  3. Choose Cura.ipa from Downloads.
  4. Keep AltStore open and your computer on, with AltServer running on the same Wi-Fi, until installation finishes.

Keeping Cura working

Free Apple developer signing expires after 7 days. Refresh before it expires. Your data in Cura stays.

  1. Make sure AltServer is running and both devices are on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Open AltStore → My Apps → Refresh All.

The 7-day limit is Apple's free developer-signing limitation, not a Cura subscription.

If sign-in fails

An error like "HTTP 503" or "Failed to sign in" usually comes from Apple's servers, often after many sign-in attempts. Wait a few hours and try once more, or use a different free Apple Account. For more help, see the official AltStore FAQ.
