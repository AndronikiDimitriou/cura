Cura

Cura is a medicine-reminder and personal health app. Everything you enter stays on your own phone.

Choose your device below to install Cura.

Android

No computer needed. No 7-day expiry.

Download Cura.apk
  1. Open the download link on your Android phone.
  2. Download the APK file.
  3. Tap the downloaded Cura.apk file.
  4. If Android asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for the app you opened it with (Chrome, Files, Gmail, etc.).
  5. Go back and tap Install.
  6. Open Cura. Done!

Android may display a security warning because Cura is installed outside Google Play.

iPhone

Free installation using SideStore. You need a computer for the initial setup.

Important: The initial installation requires a USB cable connecting your iPhone to your computer. An internet connection alone cannot replace this step.

What you need

  • An iPhone with a passcode enabled.
  • A Windows PC, Mac or supported Linux computer.
  • A USB cable that supports data transfer.
  • A free Apple Account.
  • Internet access.

Part 1 — Prepare your iPhone

  1. Open the App Store and install LocalDevVPN.
  2. Open it once and allow the VPN configuration.

Part 2 — Prepare your computer

Windows

  1. Download and install iTunes from Apple's website . Do not use the Microsoft Store version.
  2. Open iTunes once after installation.
  3. Download the Windows MSI installer from the official iloader website.
  4. Install and open iloader.

macOS

  1. Download the macOS DMG installer from the official iloader website.
  2. Open the DMG and install iloader.
  3. No separate iTunes installation is needed.

Linux

  1. Install usbmuxd.
  2. Follow the Linux-specific instructions on the iloader website.

Part 3 — Install SideStore

  1. Connect your iPhone to the computer using USB.
  2. Unlock your iPhone and tap Trust if requested.
  3. Open iloader.
  4. Sign in using your Apple Account.
  5. Select your iPhone.
  6. Choose Install SideStore.
  7. Wait until installation finishes.
  8. You can unplug your iPhone after installation.

Part 4 — Trust and configure SideStore

  1. On your iPhone, open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.
  2. Select the developer profile associated with your Apple Account and tap Trust if prompted.
  3. If requested, enable Developer Mode under Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode. Your iPhone may restart.
  4. Open LocalDevVPN and connect.
  5. Open SideStore and sign in using the same Apple Account.

Part 5 — Add Cura and install it

  1. Open SideStore.
  2. Go to Sources and tap +.
  3. Add this source:

https://andronikidimitriou.github.io/cura/sidestore.json

  1. Find Cura in the source.
  2. Tap Install.
  3. Wait for installation to finish.

Keeping Cura working

Free Apple developer signing expires after 7 days. Refresh your apps before they expire.

  1. Connect your iPhone to Wi-Fi.
  2. Open LocalDevVPN and connect.
  3. Open SideStore.
  4. Go to My Apps and tap Refresh All.

The 7-day limit is Apple's free developer-signing limitation, not a Cura subscription.

For troubleshooting, consult the official SideStore documentation .
