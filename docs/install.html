Cura

Cura is a medicine-reminder and personal health app. Everything you enter stays on your own phone.

Choose your device below to install Cura.

Android

No computer needed. No 7-day expiry.

Download Cura.apk
  1. Open the download link on your Android phone.
  2. Download the APK file.
  3. Tap the downloaded Cura.apk file.
  4. If Android asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for the app you opened it with (Chrome, Files, Gmail, etc.).
  5. Go back and tap Install.
  6. Open Cura. Done!

Android may display a security warning because Cura is installed outside Google Play.

iPhone

Free installation using AltStore. You need a Mac or Windows computer.

Download Cura.ipa
Important: The first installation needs a USB cable connecting your iPhone to your computer.

What you need

  • An iPhone with a passcode enabled.
  • A Mac or Windows PC.
  • A USB cable that supports data transfer.
  • A free Apple Account.
  • Wi-Fi, with the iPhone and computer on the same network.
